Ingredients for Cooking Jollof Rice

  • Rice
  • Vegetable Oil
  • Onions
  • Tomatoes
  • Maggi Seasoning Cubes
  • Water

Steps for Cooking Jollof Rice

Step 1
Rinse 3 cups of rice

Step 2
Boil 3 litres of water

Step 3
Add rice into the boiled water

Step 4
After 20 minutes, add onions and tomatoes into the pot

Step 5
After 10 minutes, add vegetable oil and the Maggi seasoning

Step 6
After 10 minutes, stir the food until there is consistency
